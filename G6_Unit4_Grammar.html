1. Comparative adjectives – So sánh hơn

Dùng để so sánh HAI người, vật hoặc nơi chốn.

S1 + is/are + adj-er / more + adj + than + S2

Tính từQuy tắcVí dụ
1 âm tiết+ ertall → taller, cheap → cheaper
1 âm tiết, tận cùng -e+ rlarge → larger, wide → wider
1 nguyên âm + 1 phụ âmgấp đôi phụ âm + erbig → bigger, hot → hotter
2 âm tiết, tận cùng -yy → iernoisy → noisier, busy → busier
2 âm tiết trở lênmore + adjmodern → more modern, expensive → more expensive
quiet, narrow, clever, gentlecả hai cáchquieter / more quiet
Bất quy tắchọc thuộcgood → better, bad → worse, far → farther

My neighbourhood is noisier than your neighbourhood.
Living in the countryside is more peaceful than living in the city.
Is Binh Minh noisier than Long Son? Yes, it is.

⚠️ Không dùng cả more và -er (more bigger ❌). than (hơn) khác then (sau đó).

2. Making suggestions – Đưa ra gợi ý

Let's + V  ·  Shall we + V?

Let's go to Chua Cau.
Shall we go there first?
OK, sure. Good idea!

3. Asking for and giving directions – Hỏi và chỉ đường

Excuse me. Could you tell me the way to the cinema, please?
Go along this street. It's on your left.
Where's the nearest post office, please?
Take the first turning on the right. It's next to the bank.

Nối các bước: First, … Then, … After that, … Finally, …
